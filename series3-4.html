The City of Glass

Jace grapples with his feelings for Clary and his doubts about their connection. Clary discovers new abilities, including creating a rune to open portals, which proves vital during the unfolding battles. Simon, now a Daylighter vampire, faces his own trials when he’s imprisoned by the Shadowhunters for political leverage.

The climax sees Valentine succeeding in summoning Raziel but failing to bend him to his will. Raziel kills Valentine and grants Clary one wish. She uses it to save Jace, who dies during the confrontation. The book ends with the Shadowhunters regrouping, and Clary deciding to embrace her role among them, despite the lingering mysteries of her family.

The City of Fallen Angels

The peace after Valentine’s defeat is disrupted as Clary trains as a Shadowhunter while struggling with Jace’s strange behavior—caused by nightmares and a growing emotional distance. Meanwhile, Simon faces challenges of being a Daylighter vampire with the Mark of Cain, as mysterious forces hunt him down. Shadowhunters begin turning up dead, pointing to the reemergence of a demonic threat.

The group discovers that Lilith, a powerful demon, seeks to resurrect Sebastian, Valentine’s evil son. Lilith manipulates Jace as part of her plan, leading to a dangerous confrontation. Clary takes immense risks to save him, but in a devastating twist, Jace ends up under Lilith’s control and bound to Sebastian. The book closes on this ominous note, setting the stage for greater conflicts.
